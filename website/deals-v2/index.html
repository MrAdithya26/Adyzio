Questions

What is Adyzio?

Adyzio is the deals site of ABD Innovations, Andhra Pradesh. We pick useful products from Indian stores, write a short note on each and link you to the store to buy.

Do you earn from these links?

Yes. Adyzio is an Amazon Associate and also earns through ExtraPe for Flipkart, Myntra, Ajio, Meesho, Shopsy and other stores. You pay the same price; the store pays us a small commission.

Are the prices live?

No. Each card shows the price we saw and when. Store prices change often, so always confirm on the store page before paying.

Do you show ratings or reviews?

No. Ratings stay on the store's page where they are current. We only write who the product is for.
