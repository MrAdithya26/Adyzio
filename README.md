| Place | What it holds | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **GitHub** `MrAdithya26/Adyzio` | All code, plans and notes, with every version | **Master copy.** Every change is saved here first, with a clear message. |

| Folder | What goes here |

## Rules

- Every page with affiliate links carries a clear affiliate disclosure (required by Amazon Associates).
- No invented prices, discounts or specs. Anything to check against the store page is marked **VERIFY**.
- No passwords, tokens or account numbers in GitHub. They go in `private/` on your computer only.
