Deal Scraper & Affiliate – Settings

1. Google Drive & Google Sheet (no sign-in)

Google does not let any app upload to Drive with only a shared link. So you add a small script to your own Google account once. It saves the images and writes the sheet rows for the extension.

  1. Open script.google.com → New project.
  2. Delete what is there, paste the script below (Copy script), press Save.
  3. Deploy → New deployment → gear icon → Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Deploy, then Authorize access and allow.
  4. Copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec) into the box below.
