🔗 Link finder

Find each product on each store → product link + affiliate link

1. Load your list

Excel or CSV with a Product column and store search columns named like Amazon search, Flipkart search, Meesho search. The Reels Schedule file works as it is (sheet "Schedule").

2. Find the links

A store tab opens with the search. Click the right product, then press ✓ Use this product in the dark bar at the top. It saves both links and opens the next store. Your progress is kept even if you close Chrome.

To put the links in the Schedule: open the downloaded file, copy columns C to I (from row 2 down), and in the Schedule sheet click cell M2 → right-click → Paste special → Values only.
